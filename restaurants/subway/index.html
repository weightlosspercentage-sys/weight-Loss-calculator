Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the lowest calorie bread at Subway?

The 9-Grain Wheat bread and Sourdough bread are typically the lowest calorie bread options at Subway, containing about 190-210 calories per 6-inch sub.

Q: How many calories are in Subway sauces?

Sauces like mayonnaise or Chipotle Southwest add 100-150 calories per serving, while mustard, vinegar, and sweet onion sauce add 0-40 calories.
