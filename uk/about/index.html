Meet the Team Behind Weight Loss Percentage

Weight Loss Percentage was founded with a simple mission: to provide free, accurate, dietitian-reviewed health and fitness calculators. We believe that tracking your health should be scientific, accessible, and easy to understand.

Our team is composed of registered dietitians, clinical nutritionists, and exercise physiologists who review our tools, formulas, and articles to ensure they reflect the latest medical guidelines and evidence-based research.

We do not sell fad diets or unverified supplements. Our tools are designed to empower you with objective calculations to help you take control of your health and weight loss journey safely.
