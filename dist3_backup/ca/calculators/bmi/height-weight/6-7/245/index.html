Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What does a BMI of 27.6 mean for someone 6'7" tall?

A BMI of 27.6 for a 6'7" individual classifies you as Overweight. Overweight increases risk of high blood pressure, high cholesterol, Type 2 diabetes, and joint problems. A modest weight loss of 5% to 10% can significantly reduce these risks.

Q: What is the ideal weight for someone 6'7" tall?

For a height of 6'7", the healthy weight range is 164 to 221 lbs (BMI 18.5 to 24.9). Use our BMI calculator to check any weight.

Q: How is BMI calculated for 6'7" 245 lbs?

BMI = (weight in lbs ÷ height in inches²) × 703. For 6'7" (79 inches) and 245 lbs: (245 ÷ 79²) × 703 = 27.6.

Q: How can I lower my BMI from 27.6 to a healthy range?

To reach a healthy BMI, you would need to lose approximately 24 lbs. A safe rate is 0.5 to 2 lbs per week through a moderate calorie deficit. Use our weight loss calculator to create a personalized plan.
