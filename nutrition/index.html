Evidence-Based Nutrition & Scientific Methodology

All nutrition and calorie calculators on WeightLossPercentage.com are formulated using peer-reviewed physiological research, including the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, the Katch-McArdle lean mass formula, and the standard Atwater caloric values (4 kcal per gram of protein and carbohydrate, 9 kcal per gram of fat).
