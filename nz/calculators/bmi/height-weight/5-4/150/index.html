Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What does a BMI of 25.7 mean for someone 5'4" tall?

A BMI of 25.7 for a 5'4" individual classifies you as Overweight. Overweight increases risk of high blood pressure, high cholesterol, Type 2 diabetes, and joint problems. A modest weight loss of 5% to 10% can significantly reduce these risks.

Q: What is the ideal weight for someone 5'4" tall?

For a height of 5'4", the healthy weight range is 107 to 145 lbs (BMI 18.5 to 24.9). Use our BMI calculator to check any weight.

Q: How is BMI calculated for 5'4" 150 lbs?

BMI = (weight in lbs ÷ height in inches²) × 703. For 5'4" (64 inches) and 150 lbs: (150 ÷ 64²) × 703 = 25.7.

Q: How can I lower my BMI from 25.7 to a healthy range?

To reach a healthy BMI, you would need to lose approximately 5 lbs. A safe rate is 0.5 to 2 lbs per week through a moderate calorie deficit. Use our weight loss calculator to create a personalized plan.
