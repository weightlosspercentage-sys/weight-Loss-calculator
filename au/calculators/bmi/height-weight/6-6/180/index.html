Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What does a BMI of 20.8 mean for someone 6'6" tall?

A BMI of 20.8 for a 6'6" individual classifies you as Normal Weight. A normal BMI range is associated with the lowest risk of weight-related health problems. Maintain this range through balanced nutrition and regular physical activity.

Q: What is the ideal weight for someone 6'6" tall?

For a height of 6'6", the healthy weight range is 160 to 215 lbs (BMI 18.5 to 24.9). Use our BMI calculator to check any weight.

Q: How is BMI calculated for 6'6" 180 lbs?

BMI = (weight in lbs ÷ height in inches²) × 703. For 6'6" (78 inches) and 180 lbs: (180 ÷ 78²) × 703 = 20.8.
