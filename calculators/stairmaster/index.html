How many calories do 15 minutes on the StairMaster burn?

A 150 lb individual burns approximately 140 to 180 calories in 15 minutes on a StairMaster at a moderate pace (Level 6–8).

Why does stair climbing burn more calories than walking?

Stair climbing forces your body to lift your full body weight vertically against gravity with every step, engaging glutes, quads, and hamstrings continuously.

How accurate are StairMaster console calorie displays?

Console displays often overestimate burn by 15–25% because they fail to account for handrail holding. Keeping your hands off the handles maximizes true calorie expenditure.
