How Do You Calculate Percentage of Weight Loss?

✅ Dietitian-Reviewed  |  ✅ Mifflin-St Jeor Equation  |  ✅ Updated July 2026

If you are wondering how do you calculate percentage of weight loss, the process simply requires dividing total pounds lost by your starting weight and multiplying by 100. Calculate Weight Loss Percentage Formula

Understanding the standard calculate weight loss percentage formula helps when tracking progress in spreadsheet logs or fitness challenges:

Weight Loss Percentage Calculation:
((Starting Weight − Current Weight) ÷ Starting Weight) × 100 = Weight Loss %

Calculating Body Weight Percentage Loss vs. Scale Weight

When calculating body weight percentage loss, you measure proportional change rather than simple scale weight. A 10 lb drop for a 200 lb person equals 5%, whereas for a 150 lb person it equals 6.67%.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate percentage of weight loss?

Subtract current weight from starting weight, divide by starting weight, and multiply by 100. Example: starting at 200 lbs, now 180 lbs → ((200−180)÷200)×100 = 10%.

How to calculate weight loss by percentage for fitness challenges?

Use the initial weigh-in as the baseline starting weight. Calculate percentage weight loss using starting weight minus current weight divided by starting weight times 100.

How do I calculate percentage of weight loss in kg vs lbs?

The mathematical result of a weight percentage loss calculator is identical whether measured in kilograms, pounds, or stones.

How to calculate percentage of body weight loss safely over time?

A healthy weekly target is losing 0.5% to 1% of total body weight per week (roughly 1 to 2 lbs per week).
