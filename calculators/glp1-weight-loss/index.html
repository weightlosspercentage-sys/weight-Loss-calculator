Glp1 Weight Loss: GLP-1 Weight Loss Calculator (Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound, Ozempic)

Medically Reviewed by Dr. Rekha Kumar, M.D., M.S. • Updated: August 14, 2026

Glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptor agonists and dual GLP-1/GIP receptor agonists represent a landmark advancement in obesity medicine. Medications like Semaglutide (Wegovy, Ozempic) and Tirzepatide (Zepbound, Mounjaro) enhance glucose-dependent insulin secretion, slow gastric emptying, and suppress appetite centers in the brain, resulting in clinically significant body weight reduction.

Timeline & Phase-by-Phase Progress

  • Weeks 1–16 (Dose Escalation Phase): Patients typically experience initial rapid loss of 5% to 8% body weight as doses are titrated upward monthly.
  • Weeks 17–52 (Plateau & Maintenance Phase): Rate of weight loss stabilizes to approximately 0.5% to 1.0% per week, reaching 12% to 18% total loss.
  • Weeks 52–72 (Maximal Efficacy Phase): Total weight loss percentage reaches peak median efficacy (15% for Wegovy, 20.9% for Zepbound).
Protecting Muscle Mass on GLP-1 Therapy: Rapid weight loss can cause up to 25–40% of total loss to come from lean skeletal muscle tissue. To preserve metabolic rate, clinical guidelines recommend consuming 1.2 to 1.6 grams of protein per kilogram of body weight daily and engaging in resistance exercise 2–3 times per week. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the average weight loss percentage on GLP-1 medications?

Clinical trials indicate an average body weight loss percentage of 15% for Semaglutide (Wegovy 2.4 mg) over 68 weeks and up to 20.9% for Tirzepatide (Zepbound 15 mg) over 72 weeks when paired with lifestyle modifications.

Q: What happens if GLP-1 medication is discontinued?

The STEP 1 extension trial demonstrated that patients who stopped Semaglutide regained approximately two-thirds of their lost weight over 1 year. Long-term weight maintenance requires ongoing medical management and sustained lifestyle changes.
