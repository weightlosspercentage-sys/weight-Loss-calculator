Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What does a BMI of 63.9 mean for someone 4'8" tall?

A BMI of 63.9 for a 4'8" individual classifies you as Obese Class III. Class III (severe) obesity poses the highest risk for premature mortality, severe cardiovascular disease, and complications from metabolic syndrome. Bariatric evaluation may be appropriate.

Q: What is the ideal weight for someone 4'8" tall?

For a height of 4'8", the healthy weight range is 82 to 111 lbs (BMI 18.5 to 24.9). Use our BMI calculator to check any weight.

Q: How is BMI calculated for 4'8" 285 lbs?

BMI = (weight in lbs ÷ height in inches²) × 703. For 4'8" (56 inches) and 285 lbs: (285 ÷ 56²) × 703 = 63.9.

Q: How can I lower my BMI from 63.9 to a healthy range?

To reach a healthy BMI, you would need to lose approximately 174 lbs. A safe rate is 0.5 to 2 lbs per week through a moderate calorie deficit. Use our weight loss calculator to create a personalized plan.
