⚖️ Core Weight Loss & Metabolic Science Hub

Tracking weight loss as a percentage of initial body mass—rather than raw pounds or kilograms alone—is the preferred clinical approach endorsed by the National Institutes of Health (NIH) and the World Health Organization (WHO). This silo provides evidence-based metabolic calculators, Basal Metabolic Rate formulas, daily energy expenditure estimators, and calorie deficit planners to optimize fat loss while preserving lean tissue.

Frequently asked questions

Is this calculator free to use?

Yes. Every calculator on Weight Loss Percentage is free, with no sign-up or account required.

How accurate is the result?

Results depend on the accuracy of what you enter and the published formulas the tool uses, which follow standard guidance from national health authorities. Track the trend over weeks rather than relying on a single reading.

How often should I check it?

Check whenever your weight, activity or goals change — for most people once a week is enough to see a reliable trend.
